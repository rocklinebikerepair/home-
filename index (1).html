Frequently asked questions

How do I get my bike or scooter repaired?

We work on a call-for-service basis. Call 916-824-6302 or email rocklin.pev.repair@gmail.com, tell us about your ride and what’s going on, and we’ll get you scheduled.

What kinds of vehicles do you repair?

All kinds of eBikes and personal electric vehicles, including electric scooters, Sur-Rons and Hyperscooters. If it’s electric and has two wheels, we work on it.

Do you do regular bike repair too?

Yes. We handle general bike and eBike services like tune-ups, brakes, drivetrains, tires and wheels, along with the electrical work.

What areas do you serve?

All of Placer County and Sacramento County, plus the surrounding areas. If you’re not sure whether you’re in range, just ask.

How much does a repair cost?

It depends on your vehicle and what it needs. Call or email with the details and we’ll talk it through with you.

I’m not sure what’s wrong. Can you still help?

Absolutely. Describe what you’re noticing, whether that’s a strange noise, a dead battery or a motor that cuts out, and we’ll help figure it out.
